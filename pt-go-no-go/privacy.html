PT Go/No-Go Privacy Policy

Updated October 12, 2026

Your fitness records stay on your device. PT Go/No-Go does not require an account, upload your records to Sentinel Ridge, or use advertising or analytics trackers.

Information you enter

The app stores your date of birth, sex, selected MOS and component, and any optional name and rank you enter. It also stores fitness attempts, scores, CFT times and splits, body measurements, and medical profile details you choose to enter, including exemptions and notes. This information is used on your device to calculate results, select scoring standards, and show your history. It is not sent to Sentinel Ridge. You can leave optional fields blank.

Storage and backups

Records are stored in the app's local database. Generated PDF files may remain in the app's temporary cache until the operating system clears them or you delete the app. Device or cloud backups may include app data, depending on your device settings. Backups are managed by your device or backup provider; Sentinel Ridge does not operate a backup or synchronization service.

Sharing and external links

The app does not automatically share your profile or fitness records. If you export a prefilled DA Form 5500, it contains details from the selected assessment. You choose the destination through your device's share sheet. Once shared, that copy is handled by the recipient or service you choose. Opening an external website is subject to that website's privacy practices.

Advertising and tracking

The released app does not contain advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking. It does not access your contacts, photos, location, or HealthKit records. Apple may handle App Store, TestFlight, and device diagnostics under its own privacy policies and your settings.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until the app and its data are deleted. To remove them on iPhone, use Settings > General > iPhone Storage > PT Go/No-Go > Delete App. Offload App keeps your data and does not delete it. Separately remove any exported PDFs or backups you no longer want. Sentinel Ridge cannot remotely access, recover, or delete your local records.

Contacting support

If you email Sentinel Ridge, we receive the email address, message, and attachments you send. We use them to respond and resolve your request, and retain them only as needed for support and any applicable legal obligations. Email is handled by our email provider. Do not send medical details or fitness records unless needed for your request. To request deletion of support correspondence, contact daniel@sentinelridge.tech; records that must legally be retained may be kept.

Policy scope and changes

This policy covers PT Go/No-Go, provided by Sentinel Ridge. It does not cover other Sentinel Ridge products or the website hosting this policy, which may process normal website traffic information. We will update this policy if the app's data practices change and show the revised date here.
