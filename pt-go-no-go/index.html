Army fitness scores, CFT timing, waist-to-height ratio, and saved test history.

Get help

Email daniel@sentinelridge.tech. Include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version, and the steps that caused the problem. Use fictional details in screenshots. Do not send medical records or sensitive personal information.

Change your scoring profile

Open User and choose Edit details. Your date of birth, sex, and MOS determine applicable scoring standards. Editing your profile changes the current calculator subject; it does not create a separate account or history.

View event charts

Tap an event title on AFT to open its score chart. Use the age-bracket selector to compare scoring thresholds.

Share DA Form 5500

Use the download options on WHtR for a blank form or a prefilled saved assessment. Choose where to save or send the PDF through the iPhone share sheet.

Keep or remove your records

Records are stored locally. Update the app without deleting it to preserve its data. Deleting the app removes its local records; offloading it keeps them. Exported PDFs and backups are separate. See the privacy policy for details.
